Janitorial Services for Vermillion and the Southern Metro

Vermillion Janitorial Cleaning has served commercial and industrial facilities across Dakota County for 22 years. Our service area runs from Hastings and Farmington in the south to Rosemount and Coates to the north, covering the small-business corridors, warehouse facilities, and agricultural operations that make up this part of southern Minnesota.

We do not operate from a storefront. Every hour goes into crews and materials. That keeps our overhead low and our pricing competitive against the larger regional contractors who route work through a franchise model.

Why Facility Managers in Dakota County Use Us

Most janitorial contractors in this corridor are one-person operations that disappear when a crew member calls out. We carry enough staff depth to cover absences without notifying clients. Your facility gets cleaned on schedule, or we communicate before the fact — not the morning after.

We provide a written scope of work before the first cleaning. That document defines exactly what gets cleaned, how often, with what products. It becomes the inspection standard for every visit. Disputes about what was or was not included in the contract become straightforward to resolve.
