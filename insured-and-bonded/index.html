General Liability Insurance

Vermillion Janitorial Cleaning carries general liability insurance appropriate for commercial cleaning operations in Minnesota. This coverage protects your facility against property damage and bodily injury claims arising from our work on your premises. A certificate of insurance is included with every written proposal and can be provided on request at any time during an active contract.

Employee Dishonesty Bonding

All crew members working in client facilities are covered under our employee dishonesty bond. This coverage addresses claims of theft or dishonest acts by our employees while on your property. It is a standard requirement for any professional janitorial contractor working in commercial and light industrial facilities, and we maintain it on all active staff without exception.

Workers' Compensation

We carry workers' compensation insurance as required under Minnesota law. This protects you from liability if a crew member is injured while working in your facility. Contractors who work without workers' comp coverage pass that liability to the business owner — this is one of the more significant hidden risks of using unlicensed or informal cleaning contractors.

22 Years in Business

Vermillion Janitorial Cleaning has operated continuously for 22 years. This record matters beyond the number itself: a cleaning contractor that maintains correct insurance, manages crews professionally, and resolves client problems rather than losing contracts does not stay in business for two decades. The operational history is the credential most worth checking, and ours is verifiable.

Requesting Documentation

If your facility requires proof of insurance before contract start — which most commercial property managers do — request it when you ask for your quote. We provide the certificate of insurance as a standard part of the written proposal package.
