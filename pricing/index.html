How We Price Janitorial Contracts

We do not publish flat per-square-foot rates online, and we would encourage you to distrust any contractor who does. The same 4,000 square feet in a general office and a food-processing facility have meaningfully different cleaning requirements — different products, different labor time, different frequency on specific tasks. A number without a site context is not a quote; it is a marketing claim.

What we can tell you honestly:

FactorHow It Affects Price
Square footageThe starting variable. More area takes more time.
FrequencyNightly contracts cost more per month than weekly, but often less per visit. Most commercial facilities in this corridor run 5-night or 3-night programs.
Restroom countRestrooms are the most labor-intensive area. A 2,000 sq ft office with six restrooms costs more than a 5,000 sq ft warehouse with one.
Floor typeVCT requiring periodic stripping adds to annual cost. Concrete and carpet have different maintenance profiles.
Special requirementsSecure areas, biohazard-adjacent cleaning, after-hours access restrictions and similar factors all affect scheduling and therefore price.

Typical Contract Structure

Most of our clients in the Hastings–Farmington–Rosemount corridor are on monthly flat-fee contracts. You pay the same amount each month regardless of how many cleaning nights fall in that month. This makes budgeting straightforward and eliminates disputes about calendar variations.

We do not require long-term contracts. Most clients start on a 90-day agreement and continue month-to-month after that. We have held contracts continuously for years under this structure — if the work is done correctly, the contract continues.
