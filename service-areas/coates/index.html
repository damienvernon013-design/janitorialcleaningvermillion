Serving Coates Facilities

Coates sits at the Interstate 35E interchange in Dakota County, making it a practical location for distribution and logistics facilities serving the Twin Cities metro — several large warehouses occupy the industrial parcels along the I-35E frontage road.

The Coates area along County Road 70 sees significant commercial truck traffic due to its position on the I-35 corridor, and the facilities here prioritize cleaning programs that account for heavy foot traffic and tracked-in debris from loading operations.

Because Coates has limited residential population but significant commercial floor space, demand for janitorial services is essentially driven by the logistics and light manufacturing operations at the interstate interchange, not by the town's registered population.
