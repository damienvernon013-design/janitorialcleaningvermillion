Serving Empire Township Facilities

Empire Township is an unincorporated township in Dakota County covering agricultural land and rural residential parcels between Vermillion, Farmington and Coates — commercial facilities here are largely ag-service businesses, rural veterinary practices and contractor operations.

The township's location along County Road 50 and County Road 60 places it between two growing suburban edges (Farmington to the west, Coates to the east), and some of the light commercial buildings here are positioned to serve both markets.

Empire Township sees pronounced winter conditions: snow accumulation and road salt use along the rural county roads means that facilities here track in significantly more debris per visit than comparable suburban facilities — a factor we account for in our scope of work.
