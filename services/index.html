How We Price

We do not publish flat rates online because the same square footage in different facility types has different labor requirements. A 3,000 sq ft dental suite and a 3,000 sq ft warehouse floor require different crews, different products and different time. We price from a walkthrough, not from a zip code and a square footage number.

What we can tell you: our pricing for this market is competitive with regional janitorial contractors and well below what a national franchise charges for comparable coverage. We do not pay franchise fees. That margin goes to crew wages and materials.
