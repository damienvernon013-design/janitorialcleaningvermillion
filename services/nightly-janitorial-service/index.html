What Nightly Janitorial Service Covers

Nightly janitorial service means your facility is cleaned on a fixed schedule after business hours, by a consistent crew who knows the building. The written scope of work we provide before the first clean defines every task — which floors get swept and mopped, which surfaces get wiped, how restrooms are handled, what gets restocked. Nothing is left to interpretation.

Standard tasks on a nightly contract include:

  • Empty trash and replace liners at all stations
  • Sweep, dust-mop and damp-mop hard flooring
  • Vacuum carpeted areas
  • Clean and disinfect restrooms — fixtures, partitions, floors
  • Wipe down common surfaces: desks (cleared), countertops, break room appliances exterior
  • Spot-clean glass at entry doors and interior partitions
  • Restock paper goods and hand soap where access is provided

Task frequency can be adjusted: some items run nightly, others weekly or bi-weekly, based on your facility type and traffic.

Who This Service Fits

Nightly janitorial service is right for offices, professional suites, small manufacturing and light industrial spaces, and medical-adjacent facilities where cleaning must happen outside business hours. If your facility generates daily mess — foot traffic, break rooms in use, restrooms with regular use — a nightly contract is more cost-effective than a periodic deep-clean schedule.

Facilities we commonly service in this corridor include general commercial offices in Hastings and Farmington, agricultural equipment dealers, and the small industrial units along the Highway 55 and Highway 316 corridors.

Crew Assignment and Consistency

We assign a primary crew to each contract. You will know who is cleaning your building, and so will we. When crew changes occur — which they do in this industry — we notify you in advance and provide a supervised handoff, not a surprise new face at the back door.

A supervisor performs unannounced quality checks on a rotating schedule. Results are logged. If something was missed, it gets addressed on the next visit or sooner if the issue warrants it.

Pricing

Nightly janitorial pricing depends on square footage, frequency, floor type and the number of restrooms. We do not publish flat rates because a 2,000 sq ft office and a 2,000 sq ft warehouse have different labor requirements. Contact us for a written quote specific to your facility.
