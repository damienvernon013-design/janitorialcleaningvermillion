The Practical Case

Commercial cleaning is not a complicated service to evaluate. The floor is either clean or it is not. The restroom was either stocked or it was not. The crew either showed up or they did not. The reasons to choose one contractor over another come down to reliability, communication and price — in that order for most facility managers.

22 Years of Continuous Operation

We have been cleaning commercial facilities in Dakota County for 22 years. That is not a marketing claim — it is the operational record that tells you we staff correctly, price sustainably, and resolve problems rather than losing clients to them. A cleaning contractor that cannot retain clients does not stay in business for two decades.

Written Scope on Every Contract

Most of our competitors do not provide a written scope of work. They quote a price, describe the work verbally, and leave interpretation to whatever the crew does on a given night. When something is missed, there is no document to point to. When the original contact leaves and a new manager takes over, nobody knows what was agreed. We provide a written scope before the first cleaning. It defines every task, every frequency, and every standard. Disputes become straightforward to resolve.

Fixed Crew Assignment

We assign a primary crew to each contract. The same people clean your building consistently. They learn where things are. They notice when something is out of place. They develop familiarity with your specific facility's requirements. Rotating crews produce rotating results.

No Long-Term Contracts Required

We start most clients on a 90-day agreement. After that, the contract continues month-to-month. We have held contracts continuously for years under this structure. If the work is done correctly, you do not need a multi-year commitment to stay. And if we underdeliver, you are not locked in.

Pricing That Does Not Change Without Notice

Our flat monthly pricing is fixed for the contract term. We do not add fuel surcharges, supply markups or line-item fees after the fact. If our costs change and we need to adjust pricing, we tell you 30 days in advance — not on the invoice.
